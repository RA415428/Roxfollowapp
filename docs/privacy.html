Privacy Policy - Rox Follow

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

Account information: When you sign up with Google or Email, we collect your email address and (if using Google Sign-In) your basic Google profile name and photo, to create your Rox Follow Member ID.

Order information: The public social media profile link or username you submit when placing an order (for followers, likes, or views).

Usage and device data: Basic app usage and device information collected automatically via Firebase (Google) for app functionality and stability.

Advertising data: If you watch rewarded video ads to earn coins, the ad provider (Unity Ads) may collect an advertising identifier under its own privacy policy.

We do not collect your Instagram password or any other social media login credentials. We never ask for them, and you should never enter them anywhere in this app.

2. How We Use Information

To create and manage your Member ID and coin balance; to process the orders you place and forward the target profile link to our order-fulfillment service providers so the service can be delivered; to operate rewarded ads and credit coins earned from them; and to communicate important updates about your account or orders.

3. Sharing of Information

We share the minimum data necessary with Firebase (Google) for authentication and secure data storage; Unity Ads for displaying rewarded video ads; and third-party order-fulfillment providers, who only receive the public profile link/username needed to deliver your order - never your email or login credentials. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

4. Data Security

Data is transmitted using standard encrypted (HTTPS/SSL) connections. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

5. Children's Privacy

Rox Follow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under this age.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, or deletion of, your account data by contacting us at roxfollowsupport@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the revised policy.

8. Contact Us

For any privacy questions, email roxfollowsupport@gmail.com.
